# Sky Session — artwork worlds

The original nine supplied covers/images are stored as web-optimized, otherwise unchanged WebP files in `assets/sky/`. They are projected across the surrounding sky and onto floating artwork moons. I Was Away and Streams transition into their supplied wide-format alternate artworks mid-chapter. All seven chapters blend into the next without changing the audio clock.

`sky-art.js` contains the artwork list and transparent island-atlas mapping. Replace a matching WebP to change a cover. `world-islands.webp` is a 3-column × 2-row RGBA atlas: Streams mill/records, Fire ruins, Africa golden island, Artificial Love roses/heart, Black & Gifted citadel, Tiger Call filaments. The assets have real alpha; preserve it when replacing them. The source atlas was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP with alpha retained.

`sky-route.js` controls the physical route. There are three full vertical loops with forward drift and two descending two-turn helices, plus three road gaps for cloud freefalls and landings. Camera up follows the loops through inversion; dashboard controls stay in their passenger-seat positions. The route sampler normalizes travel by local path length. Chapter joins preserve position and tangent. The windshield mist is an animated transparent shader pass scoped to the windshield canvas; it expands and breaks apart during cloud dives and transitions. Reduced motion freezes the environment and clears the mist while music continues.

Run the geometry checks with `node ride/tests/sky-route.test.mjs`. They check two full journeys for finite positions, continuous joins, smooth camera frames, six complete loops, four spiral sections, and actual inverted loop frames.

## Asset generation prompts

Built-in generation used the Streams, Thru the Fire, I Woke Up in Africa, Artificial Love, and Black & Gifted images as references:

> Create a production-quality transparent PNG environment asset atlas for an immersive psychedelic neo-soul / hip-hop sky driving game. Exactly SIX separate, complete large environmental islands in a precise 3-column by 2-row grid, wide transparent gaps between every object, each object fully contained within its equal cell. Genuine transparent background, no checkerboard. Cinematic photoreal material detail, rich light, organic silhouettes, beautiful polished surrealism, no low-poly or voxel geometry. The five input artworks are visual references for the first five objects in order. Do NOT copy or invent people or faces and do NOT include any text, titles, labels or letters. Top left: monumental floating weathered stone mill cliff pouring shimmering WATERFALLS mixed with hundreds of vinyl records and iridescent CDs, lush tiny foliage, cyan highlights, mist fading into transparency below. Top middle: dramatic floating fragment of charred brick architecture, arched opening, glowing copper cracks, gorgeous flame ribbons and airborne embers fading into transparency. Top right: majestic golden savanna floating rock island with graceful acacia silhouette, warm amber sunlight, fine geometric gold ornament on the rock, waterfall of gold dust. Bottom left: lush burgundy rose cloud sculpture with a luminous broken neon heart and a vintage microphone and vinyl disc embedded in the cloud, soft pink mist fading into transparency. Bottom middle: elegant towering art deco sky citadel made of obsidian and brushed gold, violet waterfalls, flowing gold ribbons, sophisticated. Bottom right: majestic abstract TIGER made entirely of orange and white incandescent filaments and sparks, feline head sculptural and recognizable, surrounded by subtle curling brass musical forms. Three-quarter aerial view of each island, consistent light from upper left, silhouettes clean and separated, no rectangular backgrounds, no roads, no cars, no human figures. Dense spectacular professional game art, equal-sized cells sharp at highest available resolution. Sheet will be sampled per cell as transparent parallax scenery in WebGL. 3 columns, 2 rows, full objects with generous transparent padding.

Final alpha refinement:

> Edit target: the attached six-cell environment asset sheet. Preserve all SIX environmental objects, their existing cell placement, their internal details, proportions, lighting, colors, and sharpness. Change ONLY the background: remove every part of the flat colored brown/olive/pink/gray backdrop between the six islands and replace it with genuinely transparent alpha. Keep the islands' rocks, buildings, foliage, water, fire, spark filaments, heart cloud, roses, musical forms, tiger, and gold city intact. Preserve soft partial transparency around the existing mist and sparks. Need a real transparent PNG game sprite atlas, no colored backdrop, no black or white rectangle, no checkerboard painted into image. Six intact objects in a 3x2 grid with transparent separation. Do not add anything or change the artwork.
