# Generated environment assets

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, not a CLI/API fallback. These are new environment assets, not changes to the user's dashboard image. The generated files were copied into the project and used as materials on the continuous 3D scene.

## `assets/environment/facades.png`

Production asset for photorealistic browser 3D drive through northeastern Ohio. Generate a single 2048x2048 photographic texture atlas split into exactly four equal square quadrants with no gutters, no labels or borders. Each quadrant is an orthographic perfectly front-on elevation photograph of a DIFFERENT authentic late 19th century or early 20th century small Rust Belt commercial building facade, cropped exactly to the rectangular outer facade boundaries, flat roof not visible. Upper left: red brown brick two-story storefront, cream cornice, tall dark reflective windows above and empty shop glass below. Upper right: three-story worn warm brick small factory frontage tall windows with steel frames. Lower left: two-story pale stone and tan brick corner-town shop facade without perspective, display glazing below, upper windows. Lower right: three-story dark red weathered brick residential facade with regular double-hung windows, subtle rust, stone sills. Physically believable materials, restrained authentic urban imperfections, high detail photographed on overcast daylight with soft ambient shadows, no direct sunlight or cast shadows. Fill every quadrant edge to edge with facade, no sky no street no ground no people no cars no text no logos no visible sidewalls. Texture-only asset, not a scene, not illustration. Absolutely photorealistic.

## `assets/environment/maple.png`

Production asset: one full mature northeastern Ohio deciduous maple tree in late summer, complete trunk and broad irregular green foliage canopy, isolated on genuinely transparent alpha background, photorealistic photographic cutout for a realtime driving environment. Front elevation camera at approximately trunk level, entire tree from roots to highest leaf fully inside frame, plenty of tiny organic leaf gaps showing transparency, naturally asymmetrical branches with airy green leaf clusters, subtly varied olive and deep green foliage, trunk narrow brown gray ridged bark, no autumn orange. Diffuse neutral daytime lighting, no rim glow, no cast shadow, no ground or grass or sky, no other objects, no text. Square asset at highest practical resolution. The tree should look like a real tree photograph and not a stylized tree or 3D low-poly model.
