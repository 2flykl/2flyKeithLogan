How to play
← → MOVE↑ JUMP↓ INTERACT
  1. Read the target: weights are whole w units. Net = LEFT − RIGHT. Positive tilts left; negative tilts right. The green zone is the target; the cream slider shows your current balance. The prompt pulses briefly after loading. Walk beside a different scale to read it; the fifth scale reaches the card balcony.
  2. Beside an item: Down picks it up. At either tray, Down loads it; empty-handed Down returns that tray's last item. Away from scales and items, Down places it in a clear staging area.
  3. At the center: Down or Space checks the target and LOCKS the scale when met. LEFT/RIGHT need at least the shown tilt. EVEN needs net 0 and at least 4w total. EXACT needs the signed number shown. When correct, the prompt reads “Press Down Arrow to Lock Scale.”
  4. Exact check wrong? Both trays spill to clear side areas. Walking past or jumping onto a tray never spills its items. Only an explicit incorrect EXACT check at the center spills them. Timers keep their remaining seconds; a brief visible burst warning gives you room to move.
  5. Target met? Stand at the center and press ↓ / Space to LOCK before jumping. The lift is saved permanently. Jump at the right tray to rise.

The small bottom prompt tells you exactly what Down will do. One tap, one action. Carry two light items (1–2w) or one heavier item.

Use the side legend to match each belonging to its weight. Item labels show remaining seconds only.

Timers run on the ground and in your hands. Only trays pause them; loading never refills time. Belongings arrive from her balcony in occasional pairs and rapid bursts. Their timers begin when they land. Items burst into small hazards when time runs out. Help, pause and a hidden tab freeze everything. New elevations save checkpoints and restore a heart.

Get the card from the raised balcony opposite her, then jump across. After she kicks the box, recover the glowing card from the long balcony below. It has no timer and uses its own slot. Previously locked scales stay locked. The door slam breaks the balcony returns. Head LEFT from the raised card balcony and climb the outer fire escape. On the service balcony, load one 2w belonging on the LEFT tray and lock the small lift. Cross the long skywalk, time the large hearts, and take the east return to the roof. Nearby belongings have 8-second countdowns. The service balcony saves your progress and restores one heart. She throws hearts and roses from the roof. Press Down near her on the rooftop to deliver the card.

Keyboard alternatives: A/D move · W jump · S/Space interact.
Escape returns to Playable Experiences. Use the pause button to take a break. Touch uses the same direction and action buttons.
