How to play ＋
01
Position with arrowsSteer freely in any direction. Automatic travel pauses while you steer. Sand never freezes your item.
02
Tilt, then placeSpace tilts tools. Enter anchors your item and brings in the next. Buckets stay upright. A red outline means the position is blocked.
03
Build a short-lived routePlaced tools last 8 seconds, then release their sand. The dotted drop guide helps you aim. Hold Shift to send an unwanted item offscreen.
04
Fill the glass bucketsFill a placed bucket to score and add it to the moving collection row below. Grow the row to eight collectors. Bottom buckets drift, gently repel, and empty after each capture. Keep placements above their row. Ground sand above the rim line ends the game.
