2FLY UNIVERSE — NAVIGATION STYLE V6
====================================

THIS BUILD CHANGES
- Galaxies are spaced much farther apart in the global universe.
- Galaxy visual scale now grows automatically based on the amount of content assigned to that galaxy.
- Celestial systems inside content-heavy galaxies spread outward automatically.
- Orbiting child items use wider, organized orbit radii as item count increases.
- Gray-circle navigation and blinking directional arrows are removed.
- Empty-space left click now performs a direct mouse-directed "carrot" boost.
- Right mouse hold is the thruster directly. Propulsion is immediate; there is no acceleration buildup.
- Right-mouse thrust continuously follows the live mouse position.
- When inside a galaxy, that galaxy core becomes the local navigation center.
- A soft galactic atmospheric/gravity pull resists accidental outward drift while still allowing intentional right-mouse thrust to exit.
- Galaxy threshold/perimeter presentation is thicker and entry/exit effects last longer.
- Existing sustained-thrust wormholes, high-speed galaxy hyperlapse, and outer-universe return field are retained.
- Mouse wheel and orbit speeds remain faster than the earlier slow navigation pass.

CONTROLS
- Left drag: orbit/look
- Mouse wheel: cursor-centered zoom/travel
- Left click empty space: short mouse-directed carrot boost
- Hold right mouse: instant continuous thrust toward live cursor
- WASD: secondary translation
- Q/E: vertical translation

DESIGN NOTE
A galaxy is now treated as a local gravitational environment rather than just a visual object. Once captured by its atmosphere, the camera target is gradually biased toward the galaxy core and unpowered outward drift is damped. Intentional thrust can still overcome that pull and leave.
