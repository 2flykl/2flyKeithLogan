2FLY UNIVERSE — NAVIGATION STYLE V5
====================================

This build replaces the prior flight-mode arming step with direct, mouse-led navigation.

PRECISION / EXPLORATION
- Left mouse drag: direct orbit/look with no momentum carryover.
- Orbit sensitivity increased slightly for faster response.
- Mouse wheel speed increased substantially.
- Mouse wheel zoom/travel uses the LIVE cursor position as its spatial focus instead of pulling toward Universe center.
- WASD remains available for directional travel; Q/E move vertically.

DEEP-SPACE LEAD RETICLE
- In deep space, the gray circle is replaced by a blinking three-chevron directional reticle.
- First left click places/pins the lead reticle.
- Second left click gives a short controlled boost toward it.
- After the boost, the lead reticle advances farther along the same screen-space travel vector so it does not become a dead marker underneath the camera.
- Moving the mouse before pinning lets you choose a new travel direction.

GALAXY PRECISION RETICLE
- When the camera is close to a galaxy, navigation automatically switches from the blinking lead arrows to the gray precision circle.
- Object travel has a larger minimum stand-off distance, preventing selected planets/media objects from filling the camera too aggressively.

THRUSTERS
- HOLD RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON: thrust immediately. No Spacebar arming step is required.
- While held, travel/steering continuously follows the live mouse position.
- The navigation circle/arrows disappear while thrust is active.
- Release right mouse to stop thrust and restore the correct reticle for the current navigation context.
- Sustained high-speed thrust still progresses into warp and can trigger the existing chaos-travel systems.

CHAOS / SAFETY SYSTEMS RETAINED
- Sustained warp can trigger randomized wormholes.
- Warp-speed galaxy crossings retain the hyperlapse presentation.
- The spherical outer-Universe return field remains active so a traveler cannot remain lost indefinitely in deep space.
