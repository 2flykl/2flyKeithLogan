# Revision 3 Design Intent

## The Algorithm Has Faces
The enemies are no longer generic targets. Their forms represent behaviors:
- Surveillance / Tracking: watches and follows.
- Censorship / Firewall / Filter: occupies space and blocks.
- Manipulation / Consensus: pulses rather than simply shoots.
- Data Miner: moves heavily through the road network.
- Jammer: moves unpredictably, communicating interference.
- Interceptor: fast lateral movement.
- Amplification: the large heavy unit.
- Shield: defensive presence.

The goal is to make the fight feel like 2Fly is battling systems of attention,
control and repetition, not merely robots.

## Combat philosophy
Fewer bullets. Bigger enemies. Better readability.
Enemy shots use a visible warning ring before firing and are capped at eight.
The visual pressure comes from enemy scale, pathing, symbolic behavior and the camera.

## Production sprite policy
No spider/crawler units are used.
The latest armed 2Fly sheet is treated as the current master.
All runtime enemy assets are isolated production PNGs, not the captioned reference panels.
