EBONY EYES — BOARD-FIRST VISUAL OVERHAUL V3.2

Implemented from the latest gameplay review:

1. Four contestants per play mode instead of six.
   Male-player mode: Keysha, Jade, Imani, Simone.
   Female-player inverse mode: Marcus, Andre, Malcolm, Darius.

2. Contestant target information enlarged and simplified.
   Each contestant now emphasizes three large desired-trait icons and collected/required counts.

3. YOUR PROFILE moved out of the left sidebar.
   It is now a horizontal score strip directly under the contestant row.
   The strip shows the eight category icons and how many successful collections/matches the player has earned.

4. Left sidebar removed from active gameplay.
   This eliminates a major source of eye travel and reclaims board width.

5. Right HUD reduced to useful visual instruments only:
   - Love Meter
   - Combo
   - Balloons Left
   - Board Pressure
   - Spotlight Assist
   Text-heavy Lock & Flow legend removed from active play.

6. Board and tile scaling increased.
   Geometry now accounts for the reclaimed sidebar space and allows significantly larger cells on desktop.
   Tile art has stronger saturation/contrast and larger usable surface area.

7. Razzle-dazzle polish increased without covering gameplay.
   - animated board aura
   - stronger locked/pair glow
   - illuminated completed contestant requirements
   - more prominent combo/momentum lighting
   - premium Love Meter shimmer

8. Mobile remains board-first.
   Since only four contestants are active, mobile uses two larger contestant cards per page, preserving room for the full board.

9. Gameplay systems preserved.
   Lock & Flow, diagonal/expanded matching, combo logic, Ebony Eyes wildcard behavior, balloon hazards,
   weighted Flow Director, contestant reactions, positive/negative progression, and song progression remain intact.
