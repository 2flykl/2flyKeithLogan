# I Was Away — Release Environment v10.2 Cinematic Motion

Changes in this build:
- Re-cut new sprite states with a subject-mask workflow instead of white/light-gray color removal.
- Preserves light highlights in gray jeans.
- Camera orbit no longer rotates the actor to keep his back toward the camera; the actor remains world-facing and the nearest directional sprite is chosen.
- Weighted throw variation: upright chest throw, athletic dip, and occasional low throw.
- Optional post-throw behavior: neutral, subtle tracking, or hand-over-brow lookout.
- Right-leg and left-leg directional walking states used during movement.
- Instructor now walks to a safe offset away from the catch circle rather than gliding directly.
- Additional crossfade/smear timing to reduce state popping.
- Boomerang Track Cam moved to lower-left so it cannot cover the vertical throw/catch meter.
- New cinematic distant panorama shell, rocky overlook, warmer haze, lower fog density, less repetitive ground presentation, richer cloud drift.

The environment remains a hybrid: playable ground and near props are 3D; the panorama is only the far-distance world.
