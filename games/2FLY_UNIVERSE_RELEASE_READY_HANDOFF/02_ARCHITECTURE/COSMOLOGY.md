# Canonical Information Architecture

Universe -> Galaxy (5 years) -> Region (3 per galaxy) -> Systems/Clusters -> Celestial Objects -> Artifacts.

Semantic laws:
- Stars are people, exclusively.
- Galaxies are time periods.
- Regions are narrative chapters.
- Suns are rare defining works/events.
- Planets are major content/history.
- Moons are substantial related artifacts.
- Satellites are interactive/external media.
- Asteroids are small archive artifacts.
- Nebulae are creation/formative periods.
- Supernovae are transformative events.
- Comets cross eras.
- Constellations represent meaningful connections.

Spatial UX law:
Media opens inside the Universe shell and returns to the exact prior camera state.
