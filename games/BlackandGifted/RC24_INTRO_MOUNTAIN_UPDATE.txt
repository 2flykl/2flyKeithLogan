BLACK & GIFTED — RC24 INTRO + MOUNTAIN UPDATE

Scope: Scenes 1 and 2 only. Scenes 3–9 intentionally continue from the prior playable build unchanged for later review.

SCENE 1 — DEFEAT THE MESSAGE
- Opens with no walls visible.
- First forward movement triggers all three belief walls to drop sequentially: boom / boom / boom.
- Each wall impact adds shake, debris, floor scars, irregular vector edges, and symbolic fractures.
- The player struggles against the first wall before the gift appears.
- Gift was redesigned as a floating royal black/gold present with bow, starburst and strong luminous aura inspired by the source-video gift.
- Intro uses scene1_intro.mp4. Before crowning it can dominate; after crowning the museum comes forward while the same film breathes behind it.
- After YOU CAN'T is broken, Scene 1 switches to scene1_walls_fall.mp4 for the rest of the scene.
- No framed video boxes.
- A deliberately blatant TAP FORWARD TO BREAK THROUGH prompt remains visible while crowned walls remain.
- Up Arrow and Spacebar both use the same jump function (retained from RC23.1).

SCENE 2 — THE MOUNTAIN
- mountain_wide.mp4 is rendered as a blurred deep-sky layer BEFORE the mountain geometry, so the mountain naturally masks it.
- While pushing, Mountain Wide briefly surges to the visual foreground immediately before a synchronized lightning strike, then recedes behind the mountain.
- mountain_close.mp4 occupies the open sky on the opposite/right side and does not touch the mountain.
- Mountain Close breathes in/out independently.
- Rain / lightning / video visibility intensify with push progress.
- FEAR, DOUBT, WORRY and REGRET are embedded on the slope and release/fall one by one while the boulder is being pushed, rather than waiting until the end.
- Released words remain gone.
- Boulder remains seated on mountainY contact geometry.
- Push character render is lowered for stronger foot/slope grounding.

All existing media/assets remain packaged with the game.
