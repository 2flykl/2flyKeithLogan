BLACK & GIFTED — VECTOR POLISH RC5.2 GIFT COLLISION

CRITICAL GAMEPLAY FIX — CROWN GIFT

Root cause:
- The hero's P.y value represents the FEET anchor.
- The gift pickup logic compared that feet position directly to the floating gift center.
- The gift sits near the visual apex/head/body area of the jump, so the feet could never get close enough vertically.
- Result: the character visually passed through/reached the gift, but the crown event never fired.

Resolution:
- Gift collision now uses a real approximate BODY rectangle for the uncrowned hero:
  left/right body bounds + top/bottom visible sprite bounds.
- The box also uses a proper rectangular hit area.
- Pickup only triggers while airborne, preserving the intended jump interaction.
- Collision is intentionally slightly forgiving so a normal jump into the box succeeds without pixel-perfect execution.
- After collection the gift is removed immediately, particles fire, and the crown awakening spectator event starts once.

Presentation improvement:
- Added a restrained gold pulse around the gift.
- Added a small 'JUMP INTO THE GIFT' cue while it is active.
- The gift remains at the intended original-start / jump-apex location.

All RC5.1 rendering and gameplay fixes remain intact.
