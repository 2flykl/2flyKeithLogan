BLACK & GIFTED — MUSEUM RELEASE CANDIDATE 01

Open index.html in a modern browser. Click ENTER THE EXPERIENCE to begin; this user gesture starts the included MP3 soundtrack.

Controls: A/D or arrows = move, Space = jump, 1–5 = affirmation choice.

Design locks in this candidate:
- Intro begins in black/extreme close-up and pulls out to the monumental first scene.
- Opening walls read exactly: YOU CAN"T! / YOU WILL FAIL! / GIVE UP!
- Crown gift reveals only after repeated struggle.
- Boulder push is a rain/lightning spectacle with foreground motion and storm progression.
- Leap of Faith uses adaptive BLACK / AND / GIFTED platforms; first fall can leave frame before BLACK reveal.
- GIFTED is widened dynamically toward safe ground.
- Tunnel is true black; Crown is the primary visible navigation cue.
- Alignment has continuous visible/collidable ground, time symbolism, Past/Present/Future monuments, and subtle silhouette aging/growth.
- Mirror resolves through stillness.
- Included BlackAndGiftedremix26Final.mp3 loops as the soundtrack.
