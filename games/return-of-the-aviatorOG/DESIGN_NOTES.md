# Gameplay / Balance Notes

- Core feel: a theme-park / arcade ride that carries the player forward. The player reacts; the game controls macro movement.
- Inputs stay intentionally small. Left/right positioning is the universal verb across dive, runway and driving scenes.
- Camera behavior: transitions and set pieces imply zooming/scaling without changing controls. This keeps spectacle high while cognitive load stays low.
- Fairness rules: telegraphed hazards, no instant post-hit re-hit, dynamic mercy, bounded enemy fire, capped velocity, safe-lane logic.
- Fun-factor rules: near-miss scoring, combo multipliers, FLOW meter, screen-clear special, hit shake, flashes, particles, beat-synced fallback audio.
- Weapon fantasy: musical notes are visible projectiles; first W.M.P. secured in the dive is the 808 Boomer.
- Expansion path: wire the sprite sheets into texture atlases; add Treble Bow, Snare Cannon, Sampler Cannon, Feedback Axe, Drum Machine, Mic Drop and The Grand piano tank vehicle.
