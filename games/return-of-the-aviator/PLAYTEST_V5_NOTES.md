# Return of the Aviator v5
- Freefall is true 2D movement: left/right/up/down.
- Vehicle board is the inverse framework: player can move freely over the road surface with left/right/up/down while the road scrolls toward them.
- Fire is active in every scene, including the opening cutscene.
- Every gameplay/cutscene section has destroyable Algorithm targets.
- Runway starts with 2Fly running toward the vanishing point/background, then at midpoint he turns and runs toward foreground as the boss grows out of the background.
- Vehicle scene now spawns flying bots and destroyable oncoming Algorithm vehicles in addition to road hazards.
- Boss fight includes escort bots while preserving storm arena, camera scaling and floor-collapse phase.
