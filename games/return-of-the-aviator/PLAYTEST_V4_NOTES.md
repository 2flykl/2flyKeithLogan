# Return of the Aviator v4
Clean rebuild from v2 to eliminate the v3 blank-screen regression.

Key additions:
- Cutscene interaction: repeatedly tap UP/W during opening plane attack to build ESCAPE meter and trigger 2Fly's dive.
- Cutscene never hard-locks; it can complete automatically if the player does not tap enough.
- Basic Tonearm is visible before W.M.P. unlock.
- Head-down skydive with weapon aimed downward.
- Enemy bots visibly flash/recoil when hit.
- UP/W jump mechanic for runway and boss arena.
- Runway reverses halfway; player runs toward camera while the Air Boss scales up from the background.
- Vehicle board visibly scrolls and bends through an algorithmic maze-road.
- Boss arena is an ominous digital storm night.
- Boss camera scales in/out dynamically.
- At one-third boss damage the ground fractures, then the player drops to a lower grid arena.
