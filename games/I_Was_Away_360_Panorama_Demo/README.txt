I WAS AWAY — 360 PANORAMA DEMO

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Internet is required for this prototype because Three.js and the photographic panorama are loaded from their official/CDN URLs.

CONTROLS
- Drag mouse: look 360 degrees
- Mouse wheel: zoom / change field of view
- THROW BOOMERANG: run a sample 3D return arc
- AUTO ORBIT: slowly rotates the camera
- PLACEHOLDERS: hide/show the simple player/instructor stand-ins
- RESET VIEW: reset camera

WHAT THIS PROVES
- A photographic 2:1 equirectangular image can become the main world.
- Gameplay objects can remain true 3D.
- Foreground depth cards can add subtle parallax without turning the whole scene into a conventional 3D game.
- The final I Was Away environment can replace this sample panorama with a custom 8K 360 scene.

NOTE
The people in this prototype are intentionally basic geometric stand-ins. They are not the proposed final character treatment.
