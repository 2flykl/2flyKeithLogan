2FLY UNIVERSE — NAVIGATION SCALE V9 (DEFAULT +10% / THRUSTER RESTORE)

SPATIAL SCALE
- Galaxy center spacing returned to the previous/default V7 layout and increased only ~10%.
- Reset viewpoint returned to the previous scale and increased ~10%.
- Outer-universe safety radii and starfield were reduced to match the restored world scale.

ORBIT CONTENT VISIBILITY
- Orbiting media/items are ~28% larger without expanding their orbit radius.
- Archive orbit bodies are ~25% larger.
- Featured system planet bodies are modestly enlarged.
- Nearby visitor stars are slightly larger.

GALAXY COLOR / PLATE
- While inside a galaxy, its local atmosphere/plate now carries a faint wash of that galaxy's own accent color across the interior instead of being transparent in the middle.
- The colored perimeter remains stronger than the center so distant galaxies remain visible through it.

THRUSTERS
- Right mouse remains the direct thruster control.
- Immediate thrust speed is re-tuned for the restored compact world scale.
- Galaxy focal-axis correction is suspended while thrust is actively held so it cannot cancel propulsion.
- The mild galaxy focal bias resumes when thrust is released.
- The longer V8 warp-delay behavior is retained.

OTHER
- True orbit behavior from V8 is retained.
- Outer-universe home-glimmer behavior is retained and scaled to the compact universe.
