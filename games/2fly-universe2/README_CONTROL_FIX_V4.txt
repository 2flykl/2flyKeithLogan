2FLY UNIVERSE — CONTROL FIX V4

Control separation:
- Mouse wheel: normal/default navigation speed.
- Left mouse hold/drag: orbit/look only. No thrust and no momentum carryover on release.
- Spacebar hold: the only propulsion input. Progressive acceleration to warp follows live mouse position.
- Spacebar release: reduced residual travel drift with fast damping.
- Gray selector: hidden during Spacebar thrust and restored afterward.

V4 specifically removes angular inertia from mouse drag so holding/dragging the mouse cannot feel like a propulsion or drift command.
