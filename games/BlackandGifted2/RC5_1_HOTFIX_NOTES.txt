BLACK & GIFTED — VECTOR POLISH RC5.1 HOTFIX

Critical rendering regression fixed:
RC5's drawHero() used ctx.save(), translate(), and scale() but failed to call ctx.restore().
Because drawHero() runs every animation frame, the canvas transforms accumulated continuously.
That is what produced the repeated/skewed orange glitch screen after approaching the first wall.

The fix restores the canvas state immediately after drawing the hero.

This FULL package includes the character assets and existing game assets from the RC5 full build.
No intended RC5 gameplay or museum-polish changes were removed.
