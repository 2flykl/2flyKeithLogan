BLACK & GIFTED — RC7.4 CONTINUITY + CHARACTER + MUSEUM POLISH

WHAT CHANGED
1) TUNNEL OF BLACKNESS
- Crown pickups now use ONLY two lanes: ground-path and jump-path.
- The player in darkness now reuses the CURRENT character states and is simply dimmed instead of swapping to an altered pose.

2) PAST • PRESENT • FUTURE
- Box characters no longer mirror the player's jump state by accident.
- They are now large, more dynamic hero portraits that face toward the current player.
- After upgrade, they all resolve facing toward the future.

3) UPGRADED SCEPTER
- The scepter now uses a state-aware hand anchor so it follows the right hand more naturally.
- Crowned and upgraded forms reuse the same normalized crowned body scale.

4) MIRROR
- Mirror frame reduced to a thinner edge to improve depth perception and allow closer approach.
- Face-yourself stop point moved closer to the mirror.

5) THE MOUNTAIN
- Boulder made significantly larger and more intimidating.
- Doubt words appear in the storm clouds and dissipate after release.
- Sun emerges after the boulder is moved.

6) TRANSITION CONTINUITY
- Leap of Faith inherits the Mountain palette.
- Run Free begins from the Leap palette so adjacent scenes feel more continuous.
