STREAMS · WEIGHTED RIVER SYSTEM

Core design rules
- Money/value never appears as a jumpable platform. It is collectible only.
- Value and Attention rewards are attached to real platforms; landing collects them.
- The blue Attention Ball can also appear rarely as a sinking platform itself.
- Attention raises river pressure and can slightly reduce Flow; Value improves Flow.
- The route director caps Attention streaks and empty-platform streaks, inserts recovery beats, and biases side routes toward higher risk/reward.
- No mid-air collectible ambiguity.
