STREAMS OVERHAUL - PHYSICS & RIVER POLISH

This build keeps the corrected refreshed character-state system and focuses on the river/gameplay layer.

Major changes:
- Object-specific landing surfaces instead of generic full-width invisible rectangles.
- Weight and buoyancy profiles for boomboxes, microphones, headphones, cassettes, MPCs, records, laptops, contracts, keys, and music objects.
- Standing on lighter objects too long makes them sink; leaving lets most objects recover. Fragile objects sink faster.
- Natural water-contact cues use shadows, ripples, wet waterlines, and small edge disturbances rather than obvious gray boundary ovals.
- Main upstream route now uses reachable adjacent-lane jumps only, smaller vertical gaps, wider route objects, more air control, coyote time, and slightly stronger jump physics.
- Value pickups now cycle slowly and slow further when the player approaches, so the displayed reward can be intentionally timed. Rewards visibly resolve to +1, +2, or +3.
- Attention-orb animation is slower and less visually random.
- Procedural fish now move beneath the water at different depths.
- Object presentation has improved scale, waterline submersion, shadow strength, and contact effects.

Double-click START_STREAMS_PHYSICS_POLISH.bat
