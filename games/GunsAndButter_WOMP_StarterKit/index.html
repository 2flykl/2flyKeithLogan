FIND YOUR FLOW.

Shoot cyan masters. Gold doubles points. Red × plates cost 200 points and reset your combo. Armored studio plates need sustained fire.

Chain hits within 3.5 seconds to reach ×5. Bullseyes add 50%. Missed shots and expired targets break your streak. Three acts introduce faster and more complex movement across 75 seconds.

Mouse: move to aim, click to fire. Hold for Tambourine. Right-click to focus. R reloads; 1–3 switch; I inspects the WOMP. P locks the pointer, Escape pauses. Unlocked aiming also works.

Touch: drag the range to aim; hold FIRE. Use RELOAD, SWAP, and VIEW to inspect. Landscape recommended. Keyboard: arrows aim, Space fires.

CD Double Barrel: launches spinning compact discs that chain-ricochet to nearby valid targets. Tambourine Tempest: full-auto C♯ minor note fire layered with jingle-style transients. 808 Blaster: a sub-bass pressure cannon with a wide shock radius—watch nearby hazards.
