01 / THE JINGLE ENGINE

Tambourine
Tempest.

A ceremonial instrument, recast as a precision rhythm engine. Machined brass. Black enamel. Eighteen pairs of spinning metal jingles.

THE HYBRID
Tambourine × automatic mechanism
THE PROJECTILE
Paired, domed brass zills
THE EXPERIENCE
Index. Strike. Rattle. Resonate.
Mounted to this exhibit. Aim toward the perimeter range.

VISITOR BRIEFING

Learn the rhythm.

Walk with WASD or arrow keys. Move the mouse to look. On touch screens, use the movement pad and drag the view.

Approach the Tempest and press E to inspect. Operate it from its fixed mount: hold click, Space, or FIRE. Reload with R. Leave with E or Esc.

Strike the small black center for 100 points; the outer plate earns 50. Consecutive hits build a multiplier up to ×5. Press C for a 45-second recital with moving targets.
