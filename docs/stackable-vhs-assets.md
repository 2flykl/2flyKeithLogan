# Stackable VHS asset system

Nine separate transparent WebP sprites are saved in `assets/media-rooms/vhs-art/stack-tape-1.webp` through `stack-tape-9.webp`. Three low horizontal views, three shallow angled views, and three front-readable variations share blank ivory spine labels. The website adds sharp HTML titles; no channel numbers are printed on the tapes.

The original supplied Streams, I Was Away and Thru the Fire PNGs are preserved as optimized alpha WebPs at `streams-collector.webp`, `away-collector.webp` and `fire-collector.webp`. They were not regenerated. Streams is also the VCR feature, following the latest explicit image-5 instruction. Africa remains accessible through its tape/channel and HUD chapters.

## Populate a tape

In `js/site-crt-room.js`, replace a reserved `slotDefinitions` entry such as `{title:'New Story'}` with `{projectId:'your-catalog-id'}`. Add that project and its video or clips to the existing catalog. Keep the entry position to preserve its television channel. The scene, mobile catalog, channel dial, playback and HUD all share this manifest. Until a video exists the tape shows `Release Date Goes Here` and does not request media.

## Image generation provenance
