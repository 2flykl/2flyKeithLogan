2FLY DRIVE-IN — REVIEW BUILD
===========================

The Drive-In remains a separate main-navigation page alongside VHS. It plays I Was Away, Streams, and Thru the Fire from their existing source URLs.

Exterior motion always starts with the supplied Kling clip. At each clip boundary, weighted selection favors Kling (75 base weight versus 5 for each other clip). Other clips cannot play twice consecutively. Kling may play twice consecutively, then the next clip must be different. Two muted video layers crossfade over the static scene for 1.8 seconds; they do not loop themselves. Playback pauses while the page is hidden and respects reduced-motion preference. Failed clips are excluded for the visit.

The stage is sized so its movie opening is 16:9 on desktop, wide screens, mobile, and in Theater mode. The existing video keeps its full frame and no longer has wide black side bars. The screen support fade, radio controls, three-film program, hover/touch movie controls, and VHS route remain intact.

Validation: three Node sequence/weight tests pass. Chrome browser QA confirms Kling startup, Kling double-play limit, non-Kling repeat exclusion, fitted frame at 1440x900, 1896x990, and 390x844, no horizontal overflow or JavaScript errors, station navigation across all three films, and Theater mode. Earlier browser QA covered all six supplied loop files, reduced motion, failure fallback, movie playback and controls, mobile touch, cross-tab audio, and VHS regression.
