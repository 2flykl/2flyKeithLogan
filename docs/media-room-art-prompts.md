# Room artwork generation prompts

Mode: built-in `image_gen` tool. All three outputs generated at 1536×1024 and saved in `assets/media-rooms/` as WebP at quality 86, without resizing or visual retouching. Original PNGs remain in the generation output directory.

## stereo-room.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production background plate for an interactive music website, landscape 1536x1024. Create an exceptionally realistic cozy home listening room at night, circa 2002. The central hero is a magnificent brushed silver and charcoal 2000s mini hi-fi component stereo, two substantial matching speakers with visible silver cones, blue illuminated center display and a large chrome volume knob, CD changer drawer and substantial physical buttons. The stereo sits on a rich walnut credenza, warmly lit from a shaded amber lamp at the left; shelves of CDs and softly lit wall behind, edge of comfortable upholstered chair on right, warm wood grain and subtle lived-in patina. Camera front-on, straight horizontal lines, slightly above tabletop level, symmetrical stereo taking most of the frame, not a distant room view. Entire stereo and speakers fit inside image with breathing room. Center stereo chassis occupies x=38% through 62%, y=22% through 78%; speakers flank it. The central blue screen should be dark blank glass, for a functional HTML display to be overlaid later. Leave lower 15% of image as clear wooden tabletop. The room feels personal, tactile, welcoming, expensive photographic detail, cinematic amber practical light balanced with cool blue electronics. No people. No website UI, no floating text, no typography except very tiny plausible hardware markings, no readable brand logos. This is a realistic photograph, not a flat illustration or a web mockup.

## vhs-room.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production background image plate for an interactive VHS video-room website, landscape 1536x1024. Create an exceptionally realistic photograph of a cozy 1980s/1990s home den at night. Main subject: a magnificent wide old-school FLOOR MODEL CONSOLE TELEVISION in a solid walnut wood cabinet, resting on short feet directly on a warm patterned carpet, NOT a small portable tabletop TV. Big curved CRT screen left-center of cabinet, mechanical channel knobs and woven speaker grille on the right side of cabinet. A realistic black VHS VCR rests on top of the console, with a cassette slot, little green display and physical buttons. A small stack of authentic black VHS tapes beside VCR, and a shelf of VHS tapes on far left. Warm shaded floor lamp on right, subtle wood panel wall, edge of upholstered couch on right. No people. Camera exactly front-on to television, level straight-on screen for compositing functional video. Console occupies x=15% to 86%, y=31% to 90%; screen occupies approximately x=21% to 68%, y=38% to 78%. Big CRT blank dark charcoal glass, no text or picture on screen, soft subtle reflection only, horizontal screen edges; screen opening rectangle with slightly rounded corners. Keep TV screen unobstructed. All television cabinet and feet visible, VCR fully visible, cozy surrounding room supporting the central hardware. High-end cinematic interior photography with tactile wood grain, aged plastic, fabric texture, warm practical amber lighting and a hint of cool light reflected in screen, physically believable proportions, sharp detailed hero television. Not illustration, not a website mockup, no UI, no floating labels, no readable logos, no readable text.

## cd-binder.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Production website background plate. Photorealistic straight overhead photograph, landscape 1536x1024, of an open large black leather zippered CD binder album from 2002 on a warmly lit walnut coffee table. Binder spread centered and almost fills image, left and right pages flat and symmetrical, center spine vertical at exactly 50% image width, shiny metal three-ring mechanism. Four large empty square translucent polypropylene CD pockets total: TWO pockets on each page, one above the other. Left page pockets positioned between x=9% and 43%, upper y=10% to 47%, lower y=53% to 90%; right page x=57% to 91%, same vertical positions. The empty pockets have smoky charcoal backing and subtle plastic sheen at edges, no discs, no artwork, no covers, no text, no hands. Leather edging around outer sides, stitched seams, zipper teeth, realistic tiny scuffs and soft specular highlights, tangible archival object, amber lamplight with soft shadows and sharp intricate materials. Do not render any website text, UI or typography. The blank rectangular pockets will receive real CD artwork via HTML, keep them flat, frontal and unobstructed.
